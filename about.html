Why PDFit Exists

PDFit was created around a simple frustration: turning several files into one PDF should not require an account, complicated software or an unpleasant website.

Add the PDFs and images you already have, put them in the right order and create one PDF. That's the entire job.

Private by design

PDFit processes documents inside your browser. Your files stay on your device and are not uploaded to PDFit servers.

One tool. One job.

PDFit deliberately avoids unnecessary features. It is designed for the everyday moment when several PDFs, images or photographed pages need to become one clean document.

Our approach

Fast, simple, private and useful. If a feature does not make the core task easier, it does not belong in the way.
