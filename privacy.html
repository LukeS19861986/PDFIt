Privacy Policy

Privacy is part of how PDFit works.

Files selected in PDFit are processed locally inside your web browser. PDFit does not upload, receive or store the contents of the PDF or image files you use with the tool.

Camera access

If you choose the camera option on a compatible mobile device, camera access is initiated by your browser or operating system. The captured photo is processed locally as an image input.

Website analytics

PDFit may use ordinary website analytics to understand general traffic and usage. Analytics can include information such as browser type, device type, approximate location and page interactions. Analytics does not receive the contents of the documents you process.

Third-party resources

The website may load fonts and JavaScript libraries from third-party content delivery services. Those providers may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type when those resources are requested.

Email

If you contact PDFit by email, your email address and message will be used to respond to your enquiry.
